# Geist security findings — 2026-07-13

**Status:** release-blocking audit baseline. The production `geist` CLI is currently a stub, so most bridge findings become reachable when composition work starts. `draht-acp` findings are already reachable when another ACP client launches it.

A phase may claim a finding closed only when the listed remediation and regression evidence exist. Phase 33 owns pairing transport findings; Phase 35 owns ACP, permissions, process, project trust, and review-state findings.

## Immediate posture

- Do not expose `createPairingServer()` on LAN. It currently has no hostname option and therefore cannot enforce the desired loopback posture.
- The production bridge must not accept headset connections until GSEC-04, GSEC-08, and GSEC-12 are closed. Then loopback development may use `adb reverse`; non-loopback operation remains unavailable until GSEC-04's encrypted device-bound LAN protocol is fully implemented.
- Do not advertise `draht-acp` as safely headset-permissioned until GSEC-01 and GSEC-02 are closed.
- Do not rely on approve/undo as confinement until GSEC-05 and GSEC-07 are closed.

## Full-state approval amendment required by GSEC-05

The rev 7 rule `undo = reset --hard <ref>` is insufficient because it neither captures approved uncommitted bytes nor removes untracked/ignored output. Before Phase 35 can close, the canonical spec and public copy must be amended with these decisions:

1. every agent session runs only in a Geist-managed disposable worktree;
2. approval records a durable hidden full-state snapshot/ref, including tracked and untracked non-ignored files;
3. ignored files are explicitly classified as reproducible/disposable or protected, with regressions for both classes;
4. approval/undo cancels and quiesces the agent, then holds an exclusive canonical-worktree lock through action-time review recheck, snapshot, restore, verification, and ledger commit;
5. undo takes an anchored safety snapshot, restores transactionally, and verifies the resulting state;
6. bridge restart reloads `base` and `lastApproved` snapshot identity;
7. failure leaves either the target state or the recoverable safety state, never a silent partial restore.

This audit does not silently revise the locked rev 7 decision. It makes the amendment an explicit prerequisite and security acceptance gate.
